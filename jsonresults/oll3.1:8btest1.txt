----------------------------------------
Response: The FM-6 satellite is identified as a geostationary DARS (Digital Audio Radio Service) satellite. It will use the 2320.0-2332.5 MHz DARS radio frequency band for downlink operation and X-band frequencies in the 7050.5-7072.5 MHz band for uplink broadcast transmissions.

The FM-6 satellite is described as a transparent transponder type, also known as a 'bent-pipe', which supports digital communications traffic.

It will be located at the 116.15 degrees West Longitude orbital location and will serve Sirius XM subscribers in the continental United States (CONUS - including its offshore waters) and Sirius XM Canada's subscribers in Canada using a single transponder at either of two selectable frequencies.

The satellite is part of the hybrid constellation that includes five satellites, with only two operating at any given time to maximize subscriber service quality.
