Pursuant to 47 CFR § 1.3 of the Commission's Rules, Vyvx, LLC (Licensee) respectfully requests a waiver of the filing deadline set forth in 47 CFR § 25.121(e) of the Commission's Rules and requests acceptance of the accompanying renewal application for Earth Station Call Sign KB22.

Licensee requests a waiver of the requirement that renewal applications must be filed no later than thirty days before the expiration date.  The authorization (KB22) is scheduled to expire on September 13, 2026. Under the Commission's Rules the deadline for the renewal application was August 14, 2026. Due to administrative oversight, the Licensee inadvertently failed to submit the renewal application by the required deadline. Once the oversight was discovered, this renewal application was filed.

The Licensee has continuously operated the earth station in accordance with the terms and conditions of its authorization. Grant of this waiver request and acceptance of the renewal application would serve the public interest by allowing continued operation of an existing, authorized earth station facility that has been operating in compliance with the Commission's rules.
