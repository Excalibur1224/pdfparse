## Request to Extend Special Temporary Authority

Myriota Pty. Ltd. ('Myriota'), pursuant to Section 25.120 of the Commission's rules, hereby respectfully requests a 60-day extension of its existing special temporary authority ('STA') 1 to operate its customer earth stations consistent with the parameters articulated in its underlying Myriota earth station application ('MES Application'). 2 Myriota seeks temporary authority to continue integration and testing operations for its customer earth stations prior to commercial service. Myriota seeks authority to continue to operate these earth stations for a 60day period starting on July 20, 2026.

Myriota has been operating under its initial STA without incident. Grant of this application is in the public interest as it will enable Myriota's customers to integrate Myriota's earth stations and, when ready, initiate commercial service. This will facilitate the quick and timely delivery of nationwide commercial service once the MES Application is granted.

Myriota will continue to operate on a non-interference basis. Consistent with its MES application and underlying STA, Myriota will continue to operate consistent with its previous technical showings.

For the foregoing reasons, the Commission should find that the continued authorization of Myriota's integration operations would serve the public interest and grant an STA extension for an additional 60 days.
