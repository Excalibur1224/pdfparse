Test 1: Describe satellite in terms of ID, Name, frequencies and bandwidths, Active or inactive, source or origin, orbit type
----------------------------------------
Response: The FM-6 satellite is identified as a geostationary DARS (Digital Audio Radio Service) satellite. It will use the 2320.0-2332.5 MHz DARS radio frequency band for downlink operation and has two selectable frequencies available for transponder operation.

Its uplink broadcast transmissions will utilize X-band frequencies in the 7050.5-7072.5 MHz band.

The FM-6 satellite is described as being transparent, or a 'bent-pipe', type of transponder, indicating it does not modify the signal but rather simply relays it.

It supports digital communications traffic and has an orbital location at 116.15 degrees West Longitude.

The source or origin of this information appears to be related to a request for Commission authority from Sirius XM.

As for its status, the FM-6 satellite is described as being operational in early 2012, serving as backup to FM-5, and eventually transitioning to a two-satellite geostationary constellation configuration.
